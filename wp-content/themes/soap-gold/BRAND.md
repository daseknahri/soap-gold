# Soap Gold — Brand

**Name:** Soap Gold
**Pitch / tagline:** The soaps you grew up with — their greatest stories, told properly.
**Byline:** Soap Gold Team
**Domain (planned):** soapgold.getemoji.site
**Market / locale:** UK · en_GB · Europe/London

## Idea
A warm archive for soap heritage: classic Emmerdale and Coronation Street storylines retold
properly, anniversaries marked on the day, iconic characters celebrated, and the sets,
villages and locations fans still visit. Classic TV-guide nostalgia — parchment, burgundy
and antique gold — never the sibling sites' looks (not Dales & Cobbles' green/brick, not
Storyline UK's noir/red).

## Palette (tokens in assets/soap-gold.css)
- Parchment cream paper `#F7F0DC`, soft parchment `#EFE3C8`, surface `#FFFDF6`
- Deep burgundy primary `#6E1423`, dark burgundy `#4A0E18`
- Antique gold accent `#C9A227`, dark gold (text) `#8C6A1D`, clay `#8A3324`
- Ink `#2B2118`, muted `#6B5D4F`, line `#DCCBA3`
- Footer / hero darks: `#2A1210` / `#4A0E18`, on-dark cream `#FBF5E4`
- Category moods: Classic Storylines `#6E1423` · On This Day `#8C6A1D` · Iconic Characters
  `#7A3B2E` · Anniversaries & Milestones `#2F5D50` · Sets, Locations & Tours `#4A5D7E`

## Type
Literata (self-hosted woff2) for both display headlines (700, sentence case — a serif
TV-guide feel) and body reading text. Sans (system) for meta/UI only.

## Marks
- Icon: original generated emblem — a golden vintage television framed by laurel branches
  on deep burgundy (no show logos, no cast likenesses, no ITV marks).
- Hero: original generated golden-hour cobbled village street (evokes the soaps' worlds;
  depicts no real set and carries no signage text).
- Wordmark: "Soap Gold" set in a serif face, composited with Pillow over the plates —
  never model-rendered text (playbook §1.6).

## Voice & rules
Warm, knowledgeable, fan-to-fan heritage writing. Every feature checked against the
original broadcasts, listings and contemporary interviews (two sources wherever the
record allows); memories labelled as memories, theories labelled as theories; never
fabricate storylines, quotes or dates. Unofficial fan site: no affiliation with ITV;
brand evokes, never uses, show trademarks in names or art.
